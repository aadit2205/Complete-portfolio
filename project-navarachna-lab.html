Log 01 // Research Framework Target Base: Post-Consumer & Factory Denim Scraps

Textile Matrix Exploration

Textile waste poses high structural potential: woven denim fabric features crossing warp and weft yarns in dense twill weaves with inherent multidirectional tensile strength. By stabilizing scrap denim plies through vacuum and gravity resin infusing, we developed solid composite substrates to eliminate flexible waste while converting scraps into high-performance rigid workpieces.

Key Mechanical Finding Regardless of finished wall thickness, cross-woven yarn orientation inhibits fracture propagation under torsion. Structural integrity correlates directly with fabric impregnation saturation rather than excessive binding polymer volume alone.
Log 02 // Material Formulations & Evidence Comparative Trial Matrix

Denim-Resin Composite Formulations

Through systematic iterative trials balancing resin soak volume against cure times, two standardized material variants were isolated for finished product prototyping:

Variation 1: Thin Composite

Sheet Stock // Rapid Cure
Layer Count Low-ply consolidation (fewer layers)
Resin Ratio 1:1 balanced ratio (equal fabric & resin)
Cure Tactility Flat, rigid, uniform sheet; matte engineered plastic feel
Workability High precision; shearable & vector laser cut-ready
Target Forms Bookmarks, guitar picks, pendants, geometric filigree coasters
Artifact Evidence [Thin Matrix]
Guitar Pick (Twill weave visible)
Lattice Geometric Coasters

Variation 2: Thick Composite

Billet Stock // Structural Substrate
Layer Count High-density multi-ply lamination (dense buildup)
Resin Ratio High resin saturation volume
Cure Tactility Dense, heavy structural core; resembles stabilized hardwood
Workability Machineable; drilled, routed, tap-threaded, bevel-sanded
Target Forms Eyewear / sunglass frames, stationery organizers, puzzles, earrings

Log 03 // Display Architecture Secondary Deliverable

Modular Exhibition Stand System

To present material samples and student prototypes during public makerspace showcases without utilizing virgin store-bought retail furniture, an interlocking, flat-pack tier display system was developed.

  • Material: Reclaimed scrap plywood sheets
  • Fabrication: CNC router profiling with nested cut layout
  • Joinery: Friction-slot stepped assemblies (tool-free disassembly)
  • Deployment: Multi-tier showcase utilized across citywide maker expos
